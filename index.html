About

Centiles and SDS are calculated by the LMS method against the UK-WHO growth reference: UK90 from 23 weeks gestation to 42 weeks, WHO 2006 from 2 weeks to 4 years, and UK90 again from 4 to 20 years. Validated against 5,724 reference values from the RCPCH test dataset, which originate from Tim Cole's LMSGrowth; all agree within RCPCH's tolerance of 1×10−3 SDS (median 6×10−5).

Nothing you type leaves this device. The reference tables are bundled into the page and all calculation runs in the browser — no server, no database, no analytics.

A calculation aid for clinicians, to be used alongside clinical judgement. Not a registered medical device. A single centile says far less than a trajectory: read it against the child's own chart over time.

Reference data and method derived from RCPCH rcpchgrowth-python (AGPL-3.0). UK90: Cole TJ, Freeman JV, Preece MA. WHO 2006: WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. Licensed AGPL-3.0 — source code.
