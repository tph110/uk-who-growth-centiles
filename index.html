About this calculator

Centiles and SDS (z-scores) are calculated by the LMS method against the UK-WHO growth reference: UK90 data from 23 weeks gestation to 42 weeks, WHO 2006 standards from 2 weeks to 4 years, and UK90 again from 4 to 20 years.

The implementation is validated against 5,724 reference values from the RCPCH test dataset, which originate from Tim Cole's LMSGrowth. All checks agree within RCPCH's own acceptance tolerance of 1×10−3 SDS (median difference 6×10−5).

Nothing you type leaves this device. The reference tables are bundled into the page and all calculation runs in your browser. There is no server, no database and no analytics.

This is a calculation aid for use by clinicians alongside clinical judgement. It is not a medical device and should not be the sole basis for a clinical decision. Always check the plotted point against the child's own chart and growth trajectory.

Reference data and calculation method derived from the RCPCH rcpchgrowth-python package (AGPL-3.0). UK90: Cole TJ, Freeman JV, Preece MA. WHO 2006: WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. This app is licensed AGPL-3.0 — source code.
